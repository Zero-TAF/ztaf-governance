## Context

The names nearest to this programme's ground are occupied and verified: **Agentic Zero Trust** (DrZeroTrust, paper v3.0, May 2026) and **Agentic Trust Framework** (CSA, published 2 February 2026). The programme's own name and slogan are verified unused anywhere: **Zero-TAF**, slogan **Zero Tough**. A young programme earns authority through citation hygiene and honest claims, not through contesting occupied phrases or overclaiming novelty.

## Decision

1. The framework's name is **Zero-Trust Agent Framework (Zero-TAF)**; the slogan is **Zero Tough**; the slogan may appear on the organisation surface and prose, and is never a component, clause, or grade name.
2. The claimed substance is: **in agentic systems, the trust boundary is the decision.** No Zero-TAF artefact contests, redefines, or reclaims the occupied rival phrases; differentiation is done claim-by-claim in `ztaf-landscape`.
3. Citation hygiene rules, enforced through the corrections register: dates, RFC numbers, titles, and publishers are stated only as verified; the corrections register records every correction against earlier programme statements, including the seed (Appendix A of ADR-ZTAF-000).
4. Novelty claims are limited to the residual claims of ADR-ZTAF-0001. In particular: NIST's ten agent-identity tenets (2026-10-27) are treated as the assertion layer this framework binds — never as prior art being claimed, and never as prior art being disparaged.
5. External parties are characterised by what their publications verifiably say, not by inference from affiliation; vendor-adjacency observations are labelled as observations.

## Consequences

- Landscape and marketing prose are governed by the same discipline as ADRs — claims trace to the register or carry a confidence flag.
- The organisation profile, founding paper, and adoption material repeat the same one-sentence claim, unvaried.

## What this decision is not

- Not a trademark or exclusivity claim — Zero-TAF claims no exclusivity over "zero trust" or "agent" terminology.
- Not a prohibition on critique — rivals are engaged claim-by-claim, on the record.
